Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy explains what information this website collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Who I am

This website is the personal portfolio of Shahida Bu Ali, a web developer and designer in Lahore, Pakistan. I am the person responsible for the information described here. You can contact me at shahidabuali123@gmail.com.

What information I collect

Contact form. If you send a message through the form, I collect your name, email address, the topic you choose and your message. The form stores them in a database so I can read and answer them.

Booking a call. If you book a free call, you do it on a Google Calendar booking page. Google collects your name, email address and any answers you give, and shares them with me so we can hold the call on Google Meet.

Technical data. Like any website, the hosting service may record technical data such as your IP address, browser and the pages requested, for security and to deliver the site.

Your theme choice. If you switch between light and dark theme, your choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it. It is not sent to me.

What I do not do

  • I do not run advertising or tracking cookies.
  • This site does not run analytics scripts.
  • I do not sell or rent your information.

How I use your information

I use your details only to reply to you, to arrange and hold a call or project conversation, and to keep a record of that conversation. I do not add you to a mailing list.

Services that process data for this site

  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Supabase stores contact form messages.
  • Google provides Calendar booking, Google Meet and the fonts used on the site. When your browser loads Google Fonts, Google receives your IP address.
  • jsDelivr delivers a software library that the contact form uses, so it also receives your IP address when the page loads.

These services have their own privacy policies, which apply to the data they handle.

How long I keep it

I keep contact messages and booking details for as long as I need them to answer you and to keep a record of our conversation. You can ask me to delete them at any time.

Your choices

You can ask me what information I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Write to shahidabuali123@gmail.com and I will reply. If you want to remove a booking, you can also cancel it from the Google Calendar link in your confirmation email.

Children

This website is not aimed at children under 18, and I do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If I change how this site handles information, I will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy: shahidabuali123@gmail.com.
